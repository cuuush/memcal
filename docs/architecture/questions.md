# Questions

Inferred things become questions, never facts. When evidence suggests something
but does not establish it, memcal records durable typed uncertainty instead of
guessing — and the agent asks rather than asserts.

```bash
memcal ask "Is dinner with Jordan Saturday or Sunday?"
memcal answer Q4 "Saturday — Jordan confirmed by text"
```

## Durable, not decorative

Open questions are typed state with evidence, not prompt decoration. When a new
conversation may affect one, memcal shows the question beside that evidence and
records an explicit keep, amendment, answer, or closure. A deferred question can
carry a wait condition; loose word overlap alone does not answer it.

Some observations cannot be placed yet — "that's cancelled," naming no plan and no
day. Those are kept with their evidence rather than dropped or written as an
invented event, retried as later traffic arrives, and turned into a question when
more than one plan could be the one meant.

## The nosy layer, without push infrastructure

The "Ask about" block in the brief is how curiosity works with no notification
system: questions wait in context until the next conversation. The dream pass
revisits them each night against the day's traffic, and [daytime
freshness](freshness.md) can surface a question beside new activity in its thread.
