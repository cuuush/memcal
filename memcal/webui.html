What the gate did

Every conversation, and what memcal can tell about it without a model: how much of it is you, how many people in it you already know, and how many of those turn up elsewhere in your world. Muting one keeps it archived and searchable — it just stops costing model calls.

A dream pass reads only what has arrived since the last one, grouped by the person or thread it is about. This is that grouping before any of it is sent — the same text the model will get, in the same requests it will travel in.
